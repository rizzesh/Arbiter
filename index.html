OVERVIEW & GUIDE

Clarity in Seconds

Everything you need to know about how Arbiter transforms unstructured text into actionable information.

📋
EXTRACTION

What it does

Takes messy text from posters, WhatsApp forwards, brochures, email notices, or job listings and extracts all structured fields into clean, organized cards with verified dates, fees, venues, and registration links.

🚀
HOW TO USE

How to use it

Simply paste any announcement into the box above and hit "Extract details". You can also test with the one-click sample forward. Copy the cleanly extracted card text or JSON with a single tap.

💡
WHY IT MATTERS

Why it is important

College groups and team inboxes are flooded with walls of chaotic forwards. Arbiter ensures you never miss a critical submission deadline, buried Google Form link, or key eligibility requirement.

⚙️
ARCHITECTURE

How it works

Powered by Google's Gemini AI models with strict structured JSON schema extraction. Dates are normalized relative to current time, links are validated, and urgency indicators are automatically generated.
